Guest journey

Four calm steps to a confirmed stay.

Designed from the guest's side of the screen — fast, reassuring and free of the clutter that loses bookings. No account to create, no long forms, no payment gate.

01

Search

The guest picks dates and party size. The engine instantly shows only the rooms that fit — filtered and ready, in real time.

02

Select

Room cards show photos, capacity, total price for the stay and honest availability — including gentle urgency cues when inventory runs low.

03

Details

Name, email and an optional phone number, alongside a clear summary and your policies. Nothing to pay upfront.

04

Confirm

A unique booking reference is generated and a branded confirmation email is sent automatically. The guest settles with the hotel on arrival.

A closer look

01 · The guest widget

A booking flow that feels like your hotel.

Guests move through search, room selection, details and confirmation without ever leaving your site. The widget carries your name, your colours and your policies — so the whole journey reads as one continuous, trustworthy experience.

  • Rooms auto-filter to the guest's dates and party size
  • Honest availability cues encourage prompt booking
  • Dark or light theme, matched to your brand accent
02 · The admin panel

The whole back office on one screen.

Behind the widget sits a secure management panel your team actually enjoys using — a live bookings dashboard, room and rate control, a month-by-month availability calendar, and a single settings screen for your hotel's identity and policies.

  • Arrivals today, in-house now and upcoming, at a glance
  • Override prices or close dates for peak seasons and holds
  • Search any reservation by name, reference or email
Easy integration

Live on your website in minutes.

The booking engine is built to live inside your existing site, not replace it. One line of code and you're taking direct reservations — on custom HTML, WordPress, Squarespace, Wix and more.

Floating button

A branded “Book Now” button rests in the corner of your site and opens the full widget in an elegant overlay — nothing else to build.

Inline placement

Prefer it in your own layout? Trigger the widget from any button or link on the page with a single HTML attribute.

Customisable branding

Set the button label, accent colour and widget theme so the engine reads as a native part of your property's website.
