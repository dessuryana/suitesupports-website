What it does

A concierge that never clocks off.

Every capability a guest-facing assistant should have — answering, booking and escalating — deployed across your website and messaging channels from one system.

Instant answers

Availability, room types, rates, check-in times, directions, amenities — answered in seconds, drawn from your own information, never a generic script.

24/7 availability

The chatbot never sleeps. Late-night enquiries, weekend questions and different time zones are all handled the moment they arrive — no queue, no waiting.

Booking capture

Turns interest into reservations — guiding guests through dates, room choice and details, then capturing the booking or the lead instead of losing it to a closed front desk.

Faster answers, more bookings, freed-up staff.

An always-on assistant changes the economics of guest communication — response times drop from hours to seconds, routine questions stop landing on your team, and more website visitors turn into confirmed bookings.

  • Instant replies at any hour lift guest satisfaction and cut wait-time frustration

Built for every touchpoint

Present at every stage of the stay.

The same assistant works across the whole guest journey — from the first curious question to the follow-up after checkout.

Reservation enquiries

Answers availability, room types, pricing and package questions instantly — guiding guests through to a confirmed booking without waiting on a human.

Pre-arrival

Automates pre-stay messages, collects guest preferences, confirms arrival times, and offers upgrades or add-ons before guests even arrive.

In-stay support

Handles housekeeping requests, room-service orders, local recommendations and maintenance reports through one simple chat, escalating to staff when needed.

Post-stay follow-up

Sends feedback and review prompts, loyalty invitations and personalised return offers to keep guests coming back long after checkout.
