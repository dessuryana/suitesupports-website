Core Capabilities

Everything From the Scan
to the Kitchen Pass

SuiteOrder handles the whole ordering journey — guest menu, checkout, payment, kitchen, and reporting — in one connected platform, whether you run a single café or a hotel with room service.

QR Menu Ordering

Guests scan a QR at the table, counter, or in their room and order from any phone — no app, no login. The menu is mobile-first with photos, dietary tags, allergen labels, and modifiers.

Live Kitchen Display

Every order appears instantly on the kitchen screen with a timer, table or room number, modifiers, and special instructions. Bump orders from New → Preparing → Ready → Done.

Menu Builder

Create menus, categories, products, and modifiers with images, prices, dietary tags, and availability windows. Mark items sold out in one tap. AI menu import turns a PDF menu into a live one.

Online Payments

Take secure card payments through Stripe or Xendit, or let guests pay at the counter. Swap providers per venue without touching code, with surcharge and promo-code handling built in.

Reports & Insights

Sales by date, fulfilment type, and payment method. Top-selling items, refunds, QR scan performance, and tax breakdowns — so you know what's working and what to change.

Roles & Multi-Venue

Owner, manager, staff, kitchen, and finance roles with granular permissions. Run multiple venues from one login — each venue's menu, orders, and data fully isolated.

Ways to Order

One Platform,
Every Service Style

Enable exactly the fulfilment types your venue needs. SuiteOrder detects the context from the QR — a table code opens dine-in, a room code opens room service — and routes each order the right way.

Dine-In

Scan the table QR, order, and pay — the kitchen sees the table number instantly.

Takeaway

Guests order ahead and pick a collection time — ready when they arrive.

Delivery

Capture the delivery address at checkout, with delivery fees and zones handled.

Pre-Order

Let guests schedule an order for later — ideal for events, groups, and busy periods.

Room Service

Guests order from their room and charge it straight to their folio via your PMS.

Built for Hospitality

Two Things Generic Ordering
Apps Don't Do

SuiteOrder was designed for hotels and hospitality groups — so the features that matter most in this industry are built in, not bolted on.

Hotel Room-Service Mode
Order From the Room, Charge to the Folio

Each room gets its own QR. The guest scans, orders, and the charge posts straight to their room account through your property management system — no phone calls, no dockets, no re-keying at Reception.

  • Per-room QR codes with guest surname validation
  • Automatic charge-to-room posting via PMS integration
  • Native support for SuiteSupports, Cloudbeds, and Opera
  • Revenue-centre tracking for clean accounting
  • Kitchen sees the room number and order together
No Vendor Lock-In
Your Payments, Your Providers

SuiteOrder is multi-tenant with a provider-abstraction layer, so each venue connects its own payment gateway and integrations — and can switch them without any development work.

  • Stripe or Xendit per venue, swappable via settings
  • Pay online or pay at the counter — your choice
  • Card, weekend, and public-holiday surcharge rules
  • POS webhooks for automatic kitchen tickets
  • Full data isolation between every venue
Run It From One Screen

The Merchant Dashboard
& Kitchen Display

Managers and kitchen teams work from a single, real-time system — orders, menus, payments, customers, and reports, all in one place.

Live Order Board

Every incoming order in real time — filter and search by number, customer, table, or status. Accept, update the ready time, add notes, mark complete, or refund.

KPI Home

Today's orders, revenue, average order value, open orders, refunds, and sold-out items — the numbers that matter, the moment you log in.

QR Manager

Generate, label, and print QR codes for every table, counter, or room. Track scans per code and enable or disable any of them instantly.

Customer Database

Guest profiles with order history, total spend, tags, and marketing consent — the foundation for promotions and repeat business.

Promotions

Percentage, fixed, or free-delivery promo codes with usage limits, date ranges, and minimum-spend rules — applied cleanly at checkout.

Settings & Integrations

Business hours, fulfilment options, surcharges, and order rules — plus per-venue payment, PMS, and POS integrations, all configured without code.

Built to Scale

Modern Stack,
Cloud-Native

SuiteOrder runs on a modern, production-grade stack — multi-tenant from the ground up, cloud-deployable, and ready to grow from one venue to a whole group.

Frontend
Next.js + React

Fast, mobile-first ordering and dashboard built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind — installable as a web app, no download required.

Database
PostgreSQL + Prisma

Multi-tenant data model with row-level isolation per venue. Prisma ORM, money stored in cents, GST-aware — no rounding surprises.

Payments
Stripe · Xendit

Provider-abstraction layer with webhooks and idempotency keys, so charges never double-post and providers swap per venue via config.

Integrations
PMS & POS

HTTP-based contracts for PMS (SuiteSupports, Cloudbeds, Opera) and POS kitchen tickets — new providers onboarded without a rebuild.

AI
Menu Import

Upload a PDF or photo of an existing menu and AI parses it into structured categories and products — cutting menu setup from hours to minutes.

Deployment
Cloud-Ready

Stateless, serverless-friendly deployment with environment-driven config and blob storage for images. Push to deploy, scale on demand.
