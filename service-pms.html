Smart automation

Built-in intelligence, not a paid add-on.

Four smart assistants handle the repetitive, time-consuming tasks that have always eaten your team's hours.

01 · Smart reservations

Say it in plain English. It's booked.

Type the request the way you'd say it out loud and the system creates the reservation — guest, dates, room type, rate and cashier note — in a fraction of the clicks of any legacy form.

  • “2 nights, deluxe king, breakfast, paying by the company” → done
  • Multi-room walk-ins with combined or separate confirmation
  • Auto-attaches the company / agent contracted rate
02 · Smart room assignment

The morning jigsaw, solved in seconds.

Assign rooms by your rules instantly — honouring preferences, keeping parties together, and never double-booking.

  • Accessibility needs → accessible rooms, automatically
  • VIPs to the best rooms; honours high-floor, quiet and view requests
  • Upgrades gracefully when the booked type is full
03 · Smart group rooming

Paste the list. It reconciles itself.

Paste the organiser's final rooming list and the system reconciles it against the block — names and dates updated automatically, with a preview of every change before you apply it.

  • Matches updated guest names to the right rooms
  • Reconciles per-guest arrival / departure within the block
  • Locked after check-in — safe by design
04 · Smart reporting

Ask a question. Get a live report.

The standout feature: type what you want and the system builds the report from live data — then saves it to History to re-run anytime. No IT ticket, no waiting.

  • “Rooming list for group Miramar”, “arrivals next 7 days with balance”
  • Reads live data — read-only and safe, never raw database access
  • Download any result to CSV instantly
Built to be trusted

One source of truth, with guardrails.

A hotel runs on accurate data. Every screen, every role and every transaction reads from the same source — with rules enforced behind the scenes.

No status drift

A room reads the same on the dashboard, front desk, housekeeping and reservations — no conflicting statuses, no “which screen do I trust?” moments.

Hard stops, not warnings

Can't take an occupied room out of order; can't check into an out-of-order room. Guardrails enforced at the system level — a hard stop, not a warning.

Always recoverable

If anything unexpected happens, staff see a clean recover screen — never a blank or broken page. Operations continue; your guests never feel a thing.
