Inside the platform

Two things no generic CRM can match.

AI-drafted group quotations and a built-in multi-channel campaign engine are hospitality-specific — the difference between a sales tool and a revenue platform.

01 · Sales activity

Every touchpoint, one pipeline.

Log the interaction the moment it happens and the deal moves down the pipeline with it — status, value and next step visible to the whole team, so no follow-up slips.

  • Calls, meetings, inspections, proposals and rate negotiations in one log
  • Lead to Won or Lost, with follow-up reminders and value forecasting
  • Deal history sits on the account, ready for the next conversation
02 · AI group quotation

Paste the RFQ. It's quoted.

Instead of filling the quotation form field by field, paste the group inquiry email — the system reads it and populates everything in seconds, with a reply draft alongside.

  • Extracts agent, contact, dates, room count, PAX and rate basis
  • Estimates revenue and suggests a rate, with the rationale shown
  • Drafts a ready-to-send reply and a one-click quotation summary
03 · Production & segments

See where the revenue really comes from.

Production and market-segment views read from live data — company, agent and segment ranked side by side, this year against last, so the pattern is obvious at a glance.

  • Production by company, travel agent or all accounts, ranked
  • Main and sub-segment revenue, budget tracked against actuals
  • Trend graphs and CSV export on every view — no IT ticket
04 · Campaigns

Bring past guests back, from your own data.

The campaign engine is built in, with your guest database already connected — build once, filter the audience, and send across three channels without exporting a thing.

  • Visual HTML email builder with image uploads and preview
  • SMS and WhatsApp for short, high-open-rate promotions
  • Filter by VIP status, segment or contactability; archive and re-send
