SuiteComm

From voice to resolved, in seconds.

SuiteComm keeps the whole property in sync. Staff speak or type a request, it routes to the right team the moment it's made, and an SLA timer makes sure nothing slips through during the busiest hours.

Core capabilities

Everything a floor team actually needs.

Built around the real pace of a hotel floor — not adapted from a generic ticketing tool. Every request is created, routed, tracked and closed in one place.

Tasks & tickets across departments

Every request becomes a tracked task — guest requests, room moves, defects, amenity orders — routed to the right team with location and priority already attached.

Real-time messaging

New tasks and status changes push to the target department's devices the moment they happen. Live badge counts update on their own — no refresh, no chasing.

Handover logs

Generate a structured shift-handover summary for any department in one tap — open tasks first, then completed work and patterns — so incoming teams arrive briefed, not guessing.

SLA escalation

Set a response window per task. If it isn't acknowledged in time, the department head is emailed automatically — one message per task, so urgent requests never sit unattended.

Mobile first

An installable web app that lives on any Android or iOS home screen. Speak or type from the floor, read tasks offline, and carry the whole channel in a pocket.

Audit trail

Every action is timestamped and logged — created, acknowledged, completed, escalated. When you need to know who did what and when, the full history is right there.

How it works

One tap to talk. It does the rest.

Staff speak or type in plain language. Routing, tracking, escalation and reporting all happen behind the scenes.

01 · Speak or type

Say it the way you'd say it out loud.

Tap the mic and speak naturally — "two towels, room 1205, urgent" — or type it. The request is read for department, task type, location and urgency, then routed without a form to fill.

  • Plain-language requests, no menus to learn
  • Voice degrades gracefully to text where speech isn't available
  • Location and priority attached automatically
02 · Routed & delivered

The right team, the moment it's raised.

The request lands with the correct department in real time — front office, housekeeping, engineering or F&B — with the pending badge lighting up on every device instantly.

  • Live push to every device in the target department
  • Photograph a defect and it's classified and assigned for you
  • Acknowledge and complete with an optional note — all logged
03 · Nothing missed

An SLA timer watching every request.

A background check scans for unacknowledged tasks continuously. The moment a task passes its window, the department head is emailed with full details and a direct link — no manager left chasing status.

  • Configurable response window per task
  • One email per task — escalation never spams
  • Every escalation written into the audit trail
04 · Shift handover

Every shift ends with a clean briefing.

At the end of a shift, admins and department heads generate a handover summary for any team — open items first, then completed work and notable patterns from the hours just passed.

  • Short narrative plus bulleted highlights
  • Selectable by department and time window
  • Built from live task data — no manual writing
Connected departments

Four teams, one channel.

Every inter-department request flows through a single, trackable, routed channel instead of scattered calls and threads — so the whole property reads from the same live picture.

Front office

Raise guest requests, room moves, VIP amenities and late check-outs — instantly notifying housekeeping, engineering or F&B with the guest's location and priority.

Housekeeping

Receive and action room requests with live task counts, and photograph a maintenance issue to route it straight to engineering without leaving the floor.

Engineering

Receive photo-tagged defect reports classified by category and severity — AC, plumbing, electrical, furniture — so the right technician is dispatched first.

Food & beverage

Receive in-room dining requests, setup instructions and amenity orders with full context — room, guest and priority — already filled in.
